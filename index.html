Project design checklist

How every project runs

Every project goes through these steps, in this order. No exceptions.

  • The discovery call is 30 minutes, and it belongs to the client. Keep your intro to about two minutes. The one-pager covers the Zip Kit process, so the call can stay on their goals and vision.
  • Draw between meetings, not during them. Block drawing time on your calendar the same day as each call.
  • Every meeting after the first is a review. Send the drawings ahead, collect feedback as a change list, and make the changes afterward.
  • Three rounds or fewer. If round three ends and the design still isn't settled, flag it to Penguin or sales.
  • Sven approves the full design first. Then the window and door schedule, then client sign-off pending quote.
  • The quote decides the ending. If they like it, it goes to contract and your part is done. If they want to scale down, they get one final revision, then contract.
  • Book the next meeting before you hang up. Every Monday, call or email anyone who doesn't have one on the calendar.
